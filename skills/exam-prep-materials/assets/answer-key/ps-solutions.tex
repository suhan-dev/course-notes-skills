\prob{1-1}{A biased walk}
\part{a}
A single step has $\avg{s}=a(p-q)=a(2p-1)$ and $\avg{s^2}=a^2$, so
$\operatorname{Var}s=a^2[1-(2p-1)^2]=4a^2pq$.

\part{b}
Means always add; the steps are independent, so their variances add as well:
\[
  \ans{\avg{x_n}=na(2p-1),\qquad \operatorname{Var}x_n=4na^2pq}.
\]
Check: for $p=\tfrac12$ this is the unbiased walk, $\avg{x_n^2}=na^2$.
