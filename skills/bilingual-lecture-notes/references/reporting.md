# Reporting after an update

The student reads the report on a phone, between classes. Keep it short, in
their language (Korean for this student), and complete enough that they do not
have to open anything to know what changed.

## What goes in

1. **What was added**: which lectures, as which sections (numbers and titles),
   in which part; a new part if one was opened.
2. **Counts**: figures added and total, pages per edition (EN n pp, KO m pp).
3. **Site status**: published (commit, the Pages run succeeded), or why not.
4. **Copies**: PDFs placed in the Mac course folder or the shared folder.
5. **Corrections to the source material**: every place the handout, slides or
   posted notes were wrong and the notes say something else. For each: where
   (lecture, page), what the source has, what the notes say, one line of why.
   This list goes in the report, not in the notes.
6. **Anything uncertain**: a reconstructed passage, a date you could not
   confirm, a garbled equation you rebuilt, material you left out because the
   class did not reach it.

## Form

Prose and a short list; no headings for a three-line report. An illustrative
example (Korean, for a student who reads Korean; the corrections are made up):

```
8-1, 8-2 반영했어요. 제7부(양자 통계) §23 페르미–디랙 기체, §24 보스–아인슈타인
기체로 들어갔고, 그림 3개 추가(총 67개). 영문 112쪽, 국문 115쪽. 사이트 배포
확인했고 맥 notes/에도 넣었어요.

자료에서 고친 곳:
- 8-1 4쪽: 상태밀도 앞의 계수 2가 빠져 있음 → 스핀 겹침 2를 넣어 g(ε)=… 로 씀
- 8-2 2쪽: μ<0 조건의 부등호 방향이 반대로 적힘 → 노트는 μ<ε_0
```

Do not:

- attach the PDFs to the message when the site and the Mac already have them,
  unless asked;
- describe the process (which tools ran, how many passes);
- leave out the corrections list when it is empty: say "자료에서 고친 곳은
  없어요" so the student knows you checked.
